# JMYING brand symbol

## Purpose

The JMYING symbol is a pure graphic identity asset for the professional-profile
website. Three connected planes surround a clear central aperture, expressing
evidence moving through structured review into clarity. It contains no letter,
monogram or typographic glyph.

The symbol is original to this project. It uses broad institutional-brand
principles as references and does not reproduce the distinctive devices, wordmarks
or colour signatures of Deloitte, EY, PwC or KPMG.

Final generation prompt:

> Create one original, professional, pure graphic brand symbol for a multilingual
> accounting, audit, data, and finance professional-profile website. The symbol
> must contain absolutely no letters, monograms, words, numerals, typographic
> glyphs, initials, checkmarks, dollar signs, scales, buildings, or charts.
> Concept: three precise, connected geometric planes form a calm forward pathway
> around one clear central aperture, expressing evidence moving through structured
> review into clarity. It should feel institutional, trustworthy, intelligent,
> restrained, and quietly progressive. Use a simple dark institutional navy
> (#071A33) rounded-square field with a distinctive flat geometry in warm white
> (#F7F5F0) and one restrained pale blue accent (#AFC7E5). Strong silhouette,
> balanced negative space, optical alignment, readable at 24px, vector-friendly,
> no fine detail. Flat 2D design only: no gradient, no lighting, no texture, no
> bevel, no shadow, no 3D. Avoid resemblance to Deloitte's green dot, EY's yellow
> beam, PwC's stacked warm-color blocks, and KPMG's blue outlined word boxes.
> Present only the centered icon at large scale, with generous safe area, on a
> perfectly uniform chroma-magenta (#FF00FF) background for clean background
> removal. No mockup, no surrounding text, no border outside the icon, no
> watermark.

## Export rules

- Keep the master transparent and square.
- Preserve the full rounded-square silhouette and safe area.
- Export sRGB PNGs at exact target dimensions.
- Keep the graphic out of the webpage layout; the visible navigation identity
  remains live text.
- Do not add text to the symbol.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolour individual planes or apply shadows/gradients.
- Inspect 32-, 180-, 192- and 512-pixel exports after every source change.
- Update the release revision in HTML and the manifest when any export changes.
